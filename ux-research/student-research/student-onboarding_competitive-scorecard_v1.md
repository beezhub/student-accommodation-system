## Analysis Protocol

1. Score each platform independently
2. Calculate dimension averages to identify industry baseline
3. Identify BeeZhub's position relative to baseline (below/at/above)
4. Flag dimensions where BeeZhub is >50% below average (critical gaps)
5. Identify exemplary patterns worth adopting (score = 3 from any competitor)
6. Document patterns that appear in 4+ of 6 competitors (industry standard)
7. Document patterns that appear in <2 competitors (potential differentiators)
