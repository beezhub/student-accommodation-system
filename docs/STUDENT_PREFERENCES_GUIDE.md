## Application Preferences Overview

### What Preferences Can You Specify?

When submitting your accommodation application, you can specify:

| Preference | Description | Required? |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Accommodation Type | Single, Double, Studio, or En-suite | Yes |
| Preferred Building | Your first-choice residence building | No |
| Move-In Date | When you want to start your accommodation | Yes |
| Duration | How long you need accommodation (1-12 months) | Yes |
| Special Requirements | Accessibility needs, dietary, medical, etc. | No |

### How Preferences Affect Allocation

Your preferences are used to:

1. **Filter eligible rooms** - Only rooms you're eligible for are considered
2. **Guide manual allocation** - Administrators see your preferences when selecting a room
3. **Match accessibility needs** - Students requiring accessibility are matched to compliant rooms

**Important**: While we try to honor all preferences, accommodation is subject to availability. Specifying preferences does not guarantee you'll receive your exact choice. Administrators manually review each application and select the most appropriate room.

## Preferred Building Selection

### Why Select a Preferred Building?

Selecting a preferred building:

- Indicates your desired location to administrators
- Helps staff understand your preferences during manual allocation
- May prioritize you for rooms in that building when available

### Building Availability by Institution

**Important**: You will only see buildings that serve your institution. The accommodation system links buildings to specific institutions, so your building options are automatically filtered based on where you study.

For example:
- If Building A serves only Institution X, students from Institution Y won't see it
- If Building B serves both Institution X and Y, students from either can select it
- Buildings with no institution restriction are available to all students

### Checking Building Details

Before selecting a building:

1. Review building descriptions on the accommodation website
2. Check building amenities and features
3. Note the location relative to your classes
4. Consider accessibility features if needed

### Changing Preferences Before Approval

You can update your preferences **before** your application is approved:

1. Log in to your student portal
2. Navigate to **My Application**
3. Click **Edit Preferences**
4. Update your building selection
5. Save changes

**Note**: Once your application is approved or in the allocation phase, preferences cannot be changed.

## Room Type Preferences

### Available Room Types

| Room Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Single** | Private room for one person |
| **Double** | Shared room for two people |
| **Studio** | Self-contained unit with kitchenette |
| **En-suite** | Private room with attached bathroom |

### How Room Type is Matched

When administrators allocate rooms:

1. **Room type availability** - Staff prioritize rooms matching your preferred type
2. **Alternative options** - If your exact preference isn't available, similar options may be offered

**Tip**: Select your genuine preference, but be open to alternatives. Being flexible increases your chances of receiving accommodation.

## Special Requirements

### Accessibility Needs

If you have accessibility requirements, describe them in the Special Requirements field:

**Common Accessibility Needs:**
- Wheelchair accessibility
- Ground floor access
- Accessible bathroom facilities
- Visual impairment accommodations
- Hearing impairment accommodations
- Mobility assistance requirements

**How to Specify:**
```
Example: "Require wheelchair accessible room with roll-in shower.
Ground floor preferred. Need wide doorways for powered wheelchair."
```

### How Special Requirements Are Honored

1. **Automatic Filtering**: Only rooms meeting accessibility requirements are shown to administrators
2. **Strict Enforcement**: If you require accessibility, you will only be allocated an accessible room
3. **Admin Review**: Administrators see your requirements and ensure appropriate placement

### Accessibility Compliance Guarantee

The system enforces accessibility compliance:

- If you specify accessibility needs, you will only be allocated an accessible room
- Non-compliant rooms are automatically excluded from your eligible options
- This is a hard requirement that cannot be overridden

### Gender Restrictions

Rooms may have gender restrictions:

| Restriction | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| No Restriction | Open to all genders |
| Male Only | Only male students |
| Female Only | Only female students |

**How it works:**
- Your gender (from your profile) is matched against room restrictions
- Incompatible rooms are automatically excluded from your eligible options
- You cannot be placed in a gender-restricted room that doesn't match your gender

### Year of Study Restrictions

Some rooms are designated for specific year levels:

| Example Restriction | Who Can Be Allocated |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| Year 1 only | First-year students |
| Year 1-2 | First and second-year students |
| Year 3+ | Third year and above |
| No restriction | Any year level |

**How it works:**
- Your year of study (from your profile) is matched against room restrictions
- Rooms outside your year range are automatically excluded
- This helps maintain appropriate living environments for different student groups

### Other Special Requirements

You can also specify:

- **Dietary needs**: Proximity to kitchen facilities
- **Medical needs**: Specific room features required
- **Religious observance**: Quiet times, prayer space proximity
- **Study requirements**: Quiet floor preference

## Move-In Date & Duration

### How Move-In Date Affects Allocation

Your move-in date:

- Determines when your allocation becomes active
- May affect room availability (rooms may be occupied until certain dates)
- Is used for planning and preparation

**Best Practice**: Specify your actual intended move-in date. Don't specify an earlier date hoping to secure a room faster.

### Duration of Stay

Specify how long you need accommodation:

| Duration | Typical Use Case |
|----------|------------------|
| 1-3 months | Summer programs, short courses |
| 4-6 months | Semester-based accommodation |
| 9-12 months | Full academic year |

### Fixed vs Flexible Dates

**Fixed Dates:**
- Specify exact move-in date
- Better for planning

**Flexible Approach:**
- If you're flexible, mention it in special requirements
- May increase allocation options
- Example: "Move-in date flexible by up to 2 weeks if needed"

## How Allocation Works

### Application Review Process

1. **Submit Application** - Complete your accommodation application
2. **Document Verification** - Upload required documents for review
3. **Admin Review** - Staff review your application and documents
4. **Approval** - Application approved when all requirements met
5. **Manual Allocation** - Administrator selects an appropriate room for you

### Document Verification

Before allocation can proceed:

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Student ID | Verify student status |
| Proof of Enrollment | Confirm current enrollment |
| Photo ID | Identity verification |
| Medical Documentation | For accessibility requirements (if applicable) |

**Status Updates:**
- Check your portal for document status
- Respond promptly to any document requests
- Rejected documents must be resubmitted

### Manual Allocation Process

All room allocations are performed manually by administrators:

1. **Administrator views your application** - Including all your preferences and requirements
2. **System filters eligible rooms** - Only rooms you're eligible for are shown
3. **Administrator selects a room** - Based on your preferences and availability
4. **You receive notification** - Email and portal notification with your assignment

**Why manual allocation?**
- Allows individual consideration of each student's needs
- Administrators can factor in context not captured by preferences
- Ensures thoughtful placement decisions

### Understanding Room Eligibility

When an administrator allocates a room to you, they only see rooms that match ALL of the following criteria:

| Criteria | How It's Checked |
|----------|------------------|
| **Institution** | Building must serve your institution |
| **Gender** | Room gender restriction must match your gender (or be "No Restriction") |
| **Accessibility** | If you need accessibility, room must be accessible |
| **Year of Study** | Your year must be within the room's year range (if restricted) |

Rooms that don't meet ALL criteria are automatically hidden from the allocation options.

### Receiving Allocation Notification

When allocated:

1. You receive an email notification
2. Notification includes:
   - Assigned building and room number
   - Move-in date
   - Next steps for check-in
   - Contact information for questions

3. Log in to portal to view full details

### What to Do If Not Allocated (Waiting List)

If no suitable room is available:

1. **You're placed on the waiting list**
   - Ranked by application date
   - Notified of your position

2. **Wait for availability**
   - Rooms become available as students leave
   - Waiting list is processed in order

3. **Consider alternatives**
   - You may be offered alternative accommodation
   - Can update preferences if you become more flexible

4. **Stay informed**
   - Check your email regularly
   - Monitor portal for updates
   - Contact accommodation office with questions

## Understanding Room Eligibility

### What Makes a Room Eligible?

A room is eligible for you only if ALL of these conditions are met:

```
Your Institution ──► Building serves your institution
        +
Your Gender ──────► Room allows your gender
        +
Your Year ────────► Your year is within room's year range
        +
Accessibility ────► Room is accessible (if you require it)
        =
    ELIGIBLE ROOM
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Standard Allocation**
- Student: Female, Year 2, Institution X, no accessibility needs
- Eligible rooms: Rooms in buildings serving Institution X, with gender "Female Only" or "No Restriction", and year range including Year 2

**Example 2: Accessibility Required**
- Student: Male, Year 1, Institution Y, requires wheelchair access
- Eligible rooms: Accessible rooms in buildings serving Institution Y, with gender "Male Only" or "No Restriction", and year range including Year 1

**Example 3: Limited Options**
- Student: Female, Year 4, Institution Z, no special needs
- If a building only has "Year 1-2" rooms, those rooms won't appear even if they're female-allowed

### Why Am I Seeing Limited Options?

If administrators mention limited room options, it may be because:

1. **Institution restriction** - Few buildings serve your institution
2. **Gender restriction** - Limited rooms allow your gender
3. **Year restriction** - Few rooms accept your year level
4. **High demand** - Many eligible rooms are already occupied
5. **Accessibility requirement** - Fewer accessible rooms available

**What to do:**
- Be flexible on building preference
- Contact accommodation office for guidance
- Consider off-peak application periods

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "What if my preferred building is full?"

If your preferred building has no availability:

- Administrators will allocate you to another eligible building
- Your preference is still recorded for future reference
- Contact the accommodation office if you have specific needs

**Tip**: If you're flexible about buildings, mention "No preference" to maximize options.

### "Why can't I see certain buildings in the dropdown?"

Buildings are filtered by your institution. If a building doesn't serve your institution, it won't appear in your options. This is automatic and cannot be changed - it's based on agreements between institutions and residence halls.

### "Are my special requirements guaranteed to be met?"

**Accessibility requirements**: Yes, if documented. The system ensures you only receive a compliant room.

**Preferences**: Not guaranteed. We try our best, but subject to availability.

**Medical needs**: Contact the accommodation office for specific medical requirements to ensure proper documentation.

### "Can I change my preferences after applying?"

| Application Status | Can Change? |
|-------------------|-------------|
| PENDING | Yes, via your portal |
| APPROVED | Limited changes, contact office |
| AWAITING_ALLOCATION | No changes allowed |
| ALLOCATED | No changes (request room transfer instead) |

### "How long does allocation take?"

Typical timeline:

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Document review | 3-5 business days |
| Application approval | 1-2 business days after documents approved |
| Allocation | Varies based on availability and admin review |

**Note**: Peak periods (semester start) may take longer.

### "What affects my allocation priority?"

The waiting list is ordered by:

1. **Application date** - Earlier applications are processed first
2. **Special requirements** - Documented accessibility needs may receive priority

Administrators consider your preferences when selecting from eligible rooms, but preferences don't affect queue position.

### "Can I request a specific roommate?"

If you want to share a room with someone:

1. Both must apply for accommodation
2. Note the request in Special Requirements
3. Include your intended roommate's name/student number
4. Both applications must be approved
5. Contact accommodation office to coordinate

**Note**: Roommate requests are not guaranteed and depend on availability.

### "What if I need to cancel my application?"

To cancel:

1. Log in to your portal
2. Navigate to My Application
3. Click Cancel Application
4. Confirm cancellation

**Important**:
- Cancellation policies vary by timing
- Check if any fees apply
- Once allocated, cancellation may have different rules

### "How do I appeal an allocation decision?"

If you believe your allocation is incorrect:

1. Contact the accommodation office within 5 business days
2. Provide your application code
3. Explain your concern
4. Provide any supporting documentation
5. Appeals are reviewed by senior staff

### "Why wasn't I allocated to my preferred building?"

Possible reasons:

1. **No eligible rooms** - All rooms in that building were already allocated
2. **Restriction mismatch** - Rooms didn't match your gender, year, or accessibility needs
3. **Institution mismatch** - Building doesn't serve your institution (shouldn't appear in dropdown, but possible in edge cases)

The administrator selected the best available room from your eligible options.
