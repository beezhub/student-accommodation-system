Privacy Policy

Last updated: [To be completed: the date this policy is published]

BeeZhub is a platform where students apply for accommodation with the accommodation providers that use it. This policy explains what personal information we collect about you, why we collect it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

BeeZhub is run by [To be completed: the registered name and registration number of the company that runs BeeZhub], [To be completed: its physical and postal address].

What we collect

  • Your account: your first and last name, email address and password. We store the password only as a one-way hash, so nobody can read it.
  • Your student profile: student number, phone number, date of birth, gender, institution, year of study, any special requirements you enter, and an emergency contact's name, relationship, phone number and email address.
  • Your applications: the provider and intake you apply to, your institution and course, the buildings you prefer, whether you need accessible accommodation, any special requirements, and the status of each application with the notes staff add while reviewing it.
  • Your documents: the files a provider asks you to upload, for example an ID document or passport, proof of address and proof of registration, and where a provider asks for them, a financial aid application or a medical certificate. We also keep each document's review status and the comments staff make on it.
  • Rooms and offers: the rooms you are offered, your answers to those offers, your check-in, and your place on any waiting list.
  • Messages: the notifications we show you in the app and the emails we send you.
  • Security records: a record of the actions taken in your account, with the time and the IP address they came from; failed sign-in attempts, since several in a row lock the account for a while; and web server logs, which hold your IP address, the address you requested, the time and your browser type.

Some of this is special personal information under POPIA: health or disability details you give as accessibility needs or special requirements, or in a medical certificate. We collect it only when you enter it or a provider asks you to upload it, and we use it to find you accommodation that suits you.

You need a name, email address and password to create an account. Before you can apply, your profile must include your student number, phone number, date of birth, gender and an emergency contact's name and phone number, and you must upload the documents the provider marks as required. Without these we cannot process your application.

Your emergency contact's details are another person's personal information. Please tell them that you have given them to us, and show them this policy.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, keep it secure and confirm your email address.
  • To give your application and documents to the provider you apply to, so they can review it.
  • To match you with a room and manage offers, check-in and waiting lists.
  • To tell you, in the app and by email, about your account, applications, documents and offers.
  • To reach your emergency contact if something happens to you.
  • To protect the service: to detect misuse, lock an account after repeated failed sign-ins, investigate problems and keep a record of who did what.

Automatic room matching

A provider can let the platform offer rooms automatically. The platform then ranks applications and matches them to rooms using your gender (only where a room or building is for one gender), your accessibility needs, any funding you mention in your special requirements (such as NSFAS or a bursary), your institution and your year of study. You can decline any offer, and you can contact us about any decision made this way.

Who receives it

  • The accommodation providers you apply to. Their staff see your profile, the applications you send them and your documents. A provider only sees students who applied to it.
  • BeeZhub's administrators, who run the platform and support students and providers.
  • Amazon Web Services, in its Cape Town region, which hosts our servers, our database, the documents you upload (Amazon S3) and the emails we send you (Amazon SES).
  • Grafana Labs (Grafana Cloud), which stores our application and web server logs so we can monitor and fix the platform. These logs hold IP addresses and the addresses of pages requested, and some log lines hold email addresses.
  • Google, because our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads.

Information that leaves South Africa

Your profile, applications and documents are stored in South Africa, in Amazon Web Services' Cape Town region. Two things leave the country: our logs, which Grafana Cloud stores in the European Union, and the details your browser sends to Google when it loads our fonts.

How we protect it

Everything you send us travels over HTTPS. Our database is encrypted and cannot be reached from the internet. Your documents are stored encrypted in private storage, and are only handed out, through links that expire after a short time, to you, the providers you applied to, and our administrators.

Cookies and storage in your browser

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. When you sign in, the website keeps your sign-in token and your name, email address and role in your browser's local storage, so that you stay signed in. Both are removed when you sign out. While you fill in an application, your answers on the personal details step are kept in your browser's session storage so that a refresh does not lose them. They are removed when the application is created or when you close the tab.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • See your information. Most of it is in the app, under My Profile, Applications and Documents. To ask for a copy of everything we hold about you, contact us.
  • Correct it. Edit your details in My Profile, or ask us to correct anything you cannot change there.
  • Delete it. Use Delete Account in My Profile, which removes your account, profile, applications and documents, or ask us to delete it.
  • Object to us using your information, or withdraw your consent. If you do, we may no longer be able to process your applications.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you believe we have not handled your information properly: inforegulator.org.za.
